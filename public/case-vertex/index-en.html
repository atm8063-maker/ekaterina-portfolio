Brick vs. LGSF: Why It Pays Off for Investors

A direct comparison of traditional capital construction against the industrial LGSF (light-gauge steel frame) technology. Transparent facts instead of marketing promises.

Traditional (Brick)
High Risk
Build time:
12–18 months (a long wait for return on investment).
Groundwork costs:
100% — high costs for a heavy foundation and manual labor.
Investor risks:
• Delays from weather and winter downtime
• Uncontrolled construction budget overruns
• Capital frozen for 1–2 years
• Human-error mistakes on the job site
⭐ Vertex Homes LGSF
Fast · Cost-Effective · Precise
Build time:
3–4 months — a fast capital turnaround and quick handover.
Groundwork costs:
Savings of up to 30% on the foundation thanks to the frame's light weight.
Investor advantages:
• All-season dry assembly, no wet trades
• Fixed factory project budget
• Fast capital turnaround (handover in a single season)
• Factory-robot precision to within 1mm
Pilot Project · Montenegro

Pilot Project Business Roadmap

We're seeking €200,000 in investment to deliver a pilot project in Montenegro. Five key phases ensure full legal transparency and control on the road to profit.

Phase I

Deal Structuring

  • Registering the investor's company (D.O.O.)
  • Opening a corporate bank account
  • Contributing investment capital
  • Signing the Management Agreement
Phase II

Financing and Delivery

  • Tranche 1: Asset purchase and prep (40%)
  • Tranche 2: Foundation and shell (30%)
  • Tranche 3: Finishes and utilities (20%)
  • Tranche 4: Finalization and handover (10%)
Phase III

Exit and Profit

  • Selling the finished property to a buyer
  • Calculating and distributing profit
  • Withdrawing funds (full repatriation)
  • Moving on to scale the villa pool
